Swarm Drone

A tactical drone swarm that flies in formation indoors, where satellite positioning is not available.

SHEET 01 / 05
RoleProject Leader · 7-person team
PeriodJun 2025 – Present
Status
Sim-to-real
RecognitionApproved by HAVELSAN & AFAD

What

  • Autonomous swarm operation in GPS-denied indoor environments.
  • Formation flight without satellite positioning, at low unit cost.

How

  • Modular airframe designed in SolidWorks, 3D printed.
  • Flight controller carrier board with power distribution, designed in Altium Designer.
  • Handheld remote controller PCB with RF telemetry and CRC-checked packets.
  • Own PID flight control algorithm on ESP32, no off-the-shelf flight controller.
  • Training our own AI model for autonomous flight.

Result

  • Flight-ready prototypes built entirely in-house.
  • Project approved by HAVELSAN and AFAD.
  • AI model now in sim-to-real transfer: single-drone demo first, then scaled to the swarm.
